MODEL REFERENCE

Data & accuracy

Heights can include trees and buildings. Use the model for site planning. Ground levels, boundaries and construction grades require field measurements.

Model axes run east, north and up in metres. The pin is the horizontal origin. The viewer and export use true vertical scale.

Survey from Google Earth

A screenshot supplies the outline and visible measurements. A saved KML or KMZ polygon includes the corner locations.

In your Earth project, save the polygon and export it as KML or KMZ. Use Open Earth polygon above.
